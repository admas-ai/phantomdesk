NOTHING HIDING IN THE WALLS

The rulebook

Simple rules, visible reasons, a person’s decision.

Complete the request

Address, unit, supported service category, work instructions, and preferred time must be present before approval.

Keep both possible duplicates

Matching address, unit, and category trigger a review condition. Spaces and letter case are normalized. Review each affected request and record a reason; nothing is automatically merged or deleted.

PD-DUP-001

Review a possible leak

The sample leak rule suggests higher priority. It is a demonstration rule, not an emergency assessment. A person still reviews and approves the work.

PD-LEAK-001

Approve this revision

Relevant changes break the current approval seal. Previous approvals remain in history. A new matching request also needs a new review.

Check again at download

An export preview does not authorize later changes. Download checks the latest saved revisions and blocks a stale selection.

A SMALL, LOCAL PROTOTYPE

About this desk

PhantomDesk connects an approval to the exact saved instructions a person reviewed.

Saved in this browser

Records are stored on this device in this browser. There are no accounts, shared workspaces, or server backups. Clearing browser data can remove your records.

A deliberate review step

Reviewer names are local labels, not verified identities. This prototype does not assign, send, or dispatch work. Its history is not protected against someone changing the device’s storage.

Two separate workspaces

The synthetic demo contains made-up examples. Your workspace starts empty. Resetting the demo does not change your workspace.
